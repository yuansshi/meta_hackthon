How it works

  • Muse Voice Transcribe hears you in real time and marks where each turn ends, about 0.2 s after you stop talking.
  • It speaks 25 languages: pick yours under Language, below the conversation. Earshot then hears it and answers in it.
  • Muse Spark reasons at minimal effort for voice speed, searches the web, and calls tools.
  • Tools run where they belong: weather on the server; location and reminders on your device.
  • Attach a photo and ask about it. Muse Spark answers from the image itself. Ask where something is, and SAM 3.1 finds it and outlines it.
  • Heads-up (Hands-free): Muse Spark 1.2 screens what Earshot overhears and offers help, like a reminder for a plan someone mentioned. It acts only on your yes.
  • Rewind: ask about anything said near you in the last 3 minutes. It's kept in memory only, never stored.
  • For bigger jobs ("look into…"), Muse Wonder researches in the background and speaks up when it's done.
  • It remembers what you ask it to, and asks before it changes anything, like adding a reminder.
  • Answers are spoken a sentence at a time into whatever headset you use.
  • If the hosted model is unavailable, Muse Spark Open (open weights) takes the turn automatically.
Audio streams to Meta's speech recognition only while a session is open, and no audio is stored. Sessions last up to 10 minutes. Built for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon.
